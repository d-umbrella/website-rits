What I handle

Most requests fall into one of these. If yours doesn't, ask anyway - send it in and I'll tell you honestly whether I can help.

Computer trouble

Won't start, running slow, blue screens, strange errors, frozen apps - diagnosing and fixing the everyday stuff that stops you from working.

Wi-Fi & networking

Dead connections, dropped signal, router setup, printers that won't print, and getting new devices talking to your network.

Account & login issues

Locked out of email, password resets, two-factor headaches, and untangling accounts you've lost access to.

Software setup & updates

Installing, updating, or configuring the programs you rely on, and cleaning up the ones that are causing problems.

Data backup & recovery

Setting up backups before something goes wrong, and doing what's possible to recover files after it has.

General on-call troubleshooting
